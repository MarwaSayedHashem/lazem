Privacy

Private by default. Your tasks stay on this device unless you choose to sign in.

Signed out, notes, bills, medication times, and your answers live only in this browser (localStorage, and photos in IndexedDB). They are not uploaded.

Sign in with Google is optional. If you do, tasks sync to your own Firebase database so they follow you across devices. Security rules allow only that signed-in account to read them.

Weather and USD→EGP are requested from public APIs (Open-Meteo and open.er-api.com) when you open the app. Those providers see a normal HTTPS request. The app does not send your task list with those requests.

Google Calendar and Classroom connect only if you tap them, and only to pull events and assignments you choose to import.
