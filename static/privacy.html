Privacy

Lazem is local-first. There is no account and no server-side inbox.

Notes, bills, medication times, and your profile answers are stored only in this device’s browser (localStorage). They are not uploaded to Marwa Hashem, GitHub, or a Lazem backend.

Weather and USD→EGP figures are requested from public APIs (Open-Meteo and open.er-api.com) when you open the app. Those providers see a normal HTTPS request from your network. The app does not send your task list with those requests.

If you wrap Lazem as a native app, the same rules apply: data stays on the phone unless you back the phone up yourself.
