How to use Lazem

Lazem turns a quick note into an organised task — bills, medicine, appointments, errands, school and work. Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up.

✍️ Adding a task

Type naturally in the box at the top and press Add. Lazem reads the date, time, amount and place for you. Open a task and you can correct the title, category, date, time, or amount if it guessed wrong.

  • A bill: electricity 5 Oct 850 EGP
  • Medicine with a time: blood pressure medicine 9pm
  • An errand: buy milk and bread
  • An appointment with a place: dentist 4pm @Nasr City

It understands 20 languages, including Arabic — e.g. كهربا 5 أكتوبر 850. You can also tap the 🎤 mic to speak instead of typing.

🏷️ Categories

Every task is sorted automatically into one of: Bills, Medication, Health, School, Work, Errands, or Note. Each has its own colour and icon so you can scan the list at a glance.

☀️ Your "Today" card

At the top you'll see a greeting, the date and Cairo time, plus quick stats: the weather, the US dollar rate, your focus for today (how many of today's tasks are done), and your streak of active days.

✅ Working with a task

On each task card you can:

  • Done — mark it finished (undo any time from the toast that pops up).
  • Repeat — make it recur daily / weekly / monthly. When you finish it, the next one appears automatically.
  • Remove — delete it (with an Undo option).
  • ★ Star — pin an important task to the top.
  • ⌄ Arrow — open more details (below).

📋 Task details (the ⌄ arrow)

  • Reschedule — move it to Today, Tomorrow, or Next week in one tap; or add it to your calendar.
  • Checklist — break a task into steps (great for a shopping list); progress shows on the card.
  • Location — type a place and tap Open in Maps; a 📍 pin also appears on the card. Tap it for directions.
  • Note — jot anything (account number, doctor's name…).
  • Photo — attach a picture of a bill or receipt (stored only on your phone).

🔎 Finding tasks

  • Search — find any task by typing.
  • Week strip — tap a day to see what's due that day (the badge shows how many).
  • Filters — tap Bills, Medication, Health, etc. to show one category.
  • Sort — order the list by due date, amount, or A–Z.

🔔 Reminders

Open the ☰ menu → Reminders → Enable reminders. You'll get a notification when a dose or bill is due, and you can choose to be reminded 1–3 days before. Reminders work while Lazem is open in the background.

Want a bank/installment SMS to become a reminder by itself? See Auto-add from SMS below.

💳 Money: bills, installments & budget

  • Installment plans — ☰ menu → Add installment plan. Pick ValU, Souhoola, Halan, Aman, Sympl, a credit card or your own; enter the monthly amount, number of months and first due date. Lazem creates a reminder that counts down "installment 3 of 12" and stops when it's paid off.
  • Budget — a card under your list shows this month's total spending, how much is paid, a breakdown by category, and what's due in the next 7 and 30 days.
  • Higher-than-usual alert — if a bill is much bigger than your usual amount, Lazem flags it so you can check for an error.
  • Currency converter — in the ☰ menu, convert USD ⇄ EGP using the live rate.

📥 Getting things in automatically

  • Paste from SMS — ☰ menu → paste a bill/installment message; Lazem pulls out the amount and date.
  • Auto-add from SMS — ☰ menu shows a link + steps to set up your phone (MacroDroid on Android, Shortcuts on iPhone) so that installment texts turn into reminders by themselves.
  • Outlook — ☰ menu → Outlook. Sign in once and the next two months of calendar events become tasks. The first time, paste the Microsoft app client ID shown in the setup step.
  • Import calendar (.ics) — bring in assignments/events from Google Classroom, Canvas, Moodle or any calendar file; each becomes a task with its due date (and place, if the event has one).

Lazem can't read your text inbox directly (no app on the web can) — the options above are the safe, private ways to hand a message to it.

🔗 Sharing & backup

  • Share a list — the Share button packs the current list into a link you can send (e.g. hand the grocery list to family on WhatsApp). They open it and tap to import.
  • Backup & restore — ☰ menu → export your tasks to a file, and restore them on another phone. Still no account.

📊 Insights

At the bottom, tap Insights for an activity heatmap (the days you got things done), a breakdown of your open tasks by category, and your streak.

🎨 Make it yours

  • Themes — ☰ menu → choose Classic, Dark, Rosé, or Simple.
  • Language — pick from 20 languages; the app flips to right-to-left for Arabic, Urdu and Persian.
  • Install it — add Lazem to your home screen so it opens like a normal app (Android: "Install app" in the menu, or your browser's Add-to-Home-Screen; iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).

🔒 Your privacy

Lazem is private by default. Signed out, your tasks live only on your device and nothing is uploaded. If you sign in with Google (optional, from the menu), your tasks sync to your own private database so they follow you across devices — and the security rules mean only you can read them. Weather and the dollar rate are the only things fetched, and photos stay on your phone.
